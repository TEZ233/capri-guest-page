II

From the airport to Capri

From Naples airport (NAP)

  1. Airport to the port. The Alibus costs about €5 and the ride is short. A taxi to Molo Beverello is a fixed fare, roughly €24 to €28, so ask for the fixed fare before you get in. Alilauro also runs an airport shuttle for about €10 with a ferry ticket (April to October, book ahead).
  2. Port to Capri. Fast ferries and hydrofoils leave from Molo Beverello and take about 50 minutes, roughly €22 to €31 one way. A slower Caremar ferry leaves from nearby Calata Porta di Massa, takes about 85 minutes, and costs roughly €14 to €19.
  3. Marina Grande to Capri town. Boats arrive at Marina Grande. The funicular up to town takes about 4 minutes and costs about €2.40 at the ticket office (€2.90 on board).

From Rome airport (FCO)

  1. Airport to Roma Termini. The Leonardo Express runs about every 15 minutes, takes 32 minutes, and costs €14.
  2. Rome to Naples. High-speed trains (Frecciarossa or Italo) take roughly 1 hour 10 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes. Advance fares start around €9 and rise closer to the day, so book ahead.
  3. Naples to Capri. Take a taxi or local transport from Napoli Centrale to the port, then follow the Naples steps above.

Good to know

  • Fares for next September are opening now, so it's a good time to start watching prices.

  • Allow about 90 minutes between landing in Naples and your boat.
